01 Research profile

I study how symmetry shapes magnetic order and electronic structure in quantum materials. My research combines spin-space-group theory, symmetry-based modeling, and first-principles calculations to classify unconventional magnets, understand their spin textures, and identify candidate materials.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from the Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2026, following undergraduate studies in Physics at Wuhan University. My work spans the enumeration and representation theory of spin-space groups, unconventional magnetism, and symmetry-controlled topological and transport phenomena.

Spin-space groups & representation theory

Symmetry classification of magnetic structures; irreducible representations; symmetry constraints on electronic and magnon bands.

Unconventional magnetism

Altermagnets, coplanar even-wave phases, and the relationship between real-space magnetic order and momentum-space spin polarization.

Topological materials & transport

Magnetic symmetry breaking, Dirac and Weyl states, anomalous Hall responses, and magnetic breakdown.

Computational materials discovery

First-principles electronic-structure calculations and crystallographic screening guided by magnetic symmetry.

03 Selected research contributions

  • A symmetry framework for magnetic materials. Co-developed the enumeration of spin-space groups and the construction of their irreducible representations, connecting magnetic structures to symmetry-protected band degeneracies and effective models. PRX 2024 · PRB 2025
  • Classification and prediction of unconventional magnetic phases. Developed a unified classification of magnetic orders and predicted coplanar even-wave magnetism. Recent work extends this approach to the prediction of unconventional magnetic character from crystal symmetry. PRX 2026 · Preprint 2026
  • Electronic structure of a metallic altermagnet. Contributed density-functional-theory calculations to the identification of room-temperature d-wave altermagnetism in KV₂Se₂O, in collaboration with experimental teams. Nature Physics 2025
